What is TRE?

    
TRE stands for Tension & Trauma Releasing Exercises. It's a short series of simple stretches that tire out the leg and hip muscles, then you lie down and let your body gently shake or tremble on its own.

    
    
      
  • That shaking is a natural way the body lets go of stress and built-up tension.
    • 
      
  • No experience needed. You stay in control and can stop any time.
    • 
      
  • Wear comfy clothes and bring a mat or blanket if you have one.
